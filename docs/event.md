## About the Event

DISHA 3.0 is an initiative by the Rotaract Club of Galgotias Educational Institutions designed to bring students closer to the worlds of technology, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

The event aims to create a platform where students can learn from experienced professionals, explore emerging opportunities, exchange ideas, and gain practical insights into the evolving professional landscape.

Through engaging discussions, speaker interactions, and interactive activities, DISHA 3.0 aspires to help students discover new perspectives and find direction in their academic and professional journeys.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Technology:** Exploring emerging technologies and their real-world applications.
- **Innovation:** Understanding how ideas become meaningful solutions.
- **Entrepreneurship:** Learning about startups, business building, and the founder journey.
- **Career Exploration:** Discovering professional opportunities and industry expectations.
- **Networking:** Connecting students with professionals and like-minded peers.
